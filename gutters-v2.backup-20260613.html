Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do gutter guards actually work in heavy rain?
Yes. Our stainless steel micro mesh handles 22+ inches of rain per hour — far more than any South Jersey storm produces. We've installed on 1,000+ homes with zero overflow issues, including through Nor'easters and tropical storms.
Will you need to replace my existing gutters?
No. Our guards install directly onto your existing gutters in most cases. During the free assessment, Alex will inspect your gutters and let you know if they're in good shape for guard installation.
How does CleanGutters compare to LeafFilter?
Same stainless steel micro mesh technology. The difference: Alex (the owner) installs every job personally — not subcontractors. Our warranty is transferable (LeafFilter's isn't). And we typically cost 40–60% less because we don't have national franchise overhead.
What if my gutters clog after installation?
They won't — but if they ever do, we'll clean them for free and refund your installation cost. That's our lifetime money-back guarantee. No fine print, no games. The warranty transfers to the next homeowner when you sell.
How long does installation take?
Most homes are completed in one day. Alex works efficiently and leaves your property clean. You don't need to be home for the entire installation — just the initial walkthrough and final inspection.
Do you offer financing?
Yes. We offer flexible payment plans so you can protect your home now and pay over time. Ask about financing options during your free estimate — no credit impact for the quote.
